# Venue closures and refunds

A new full-day holiday closes the venue's service date and records the affected, unstarted reservations in the same MongoDB transaction. Overnight reservations follow the configured operating day: for a 07:00–02:00 schedule, a 01:00 booking belongs to the previous service date. Started and completed sessions are left intact. Existing holiday records are not retrospectively changed or notified.

The admin sees the affected guests before closing the date. Each reservation receives a persisted personal notification and an email delivery job. Online reservations use their account owner regardless of account role; staff-entered reservations link only to a verified account with the exact guest email, never to staff just because they entered the reservation. Guests without an account receive email where available and arrange their choice through staff. Guests without an email need staff contact by phone; the affected reservation remains visible in Bookings. Reading the notification API repairs previously unassigned notices for that reservation owner, preserving read state and email delivery history. Opening the bell also refreshes it immediately.

Reservation emails use the bundled `assets/riverview-logo.png` as an inline attachment, with a reservation snapshot, clear closure choices, and an action link. `refund_attention` ("Refund needs staff assistance") is a **bell-only** update. New jobs skip its email, and older queued assistance emails are marked skipped by the delivery worker. Closure, reschedule, reopening, refund-request, and completion emails continue.

Affected guests can reschedule to a future, open, available slot without the usual 24-hour cutoff or two-change limit, and without incrementing their normal allowance. Repricing and capacity rules still apply. A pending closure prevents no-show expiry, completion and session start. Removing a closure reopens upcoming unresolved reservations and sends an update. Reservations whose start has passed retain their resolution options, and accepted refunds or completed reschedules are not undone.

Choosing a closure refund cancels the reservation and approves the full remaining amount collected. The original deposit and paid total remain unchanged; only verified returned amounts increase `refundedAmount`. Ordinary customer cancellations retain the existing staff review and first-hour policy.

## Activate on the deployed backend

1. Keep the existing payment credentials and enable the provider's refund API permissions. PayMongo requires sufficient available balance; supported methods and refund windows depend on the original payment. An unsupported, expired or explicitly rejected refund is sent to staff assistance, with no fabricated returned amount.
2. Enable PayMongo `payment.paid`, `payment.refund.updated` and `payment.refunded` on `/api/payments/paymongo/webhook`. This app uses Payment Intents, so `checkout_session.payment.paid` does not replace `payment.paid`; other existing subscriptions can be preserved. PayMongo's refund events are `payment.refund.updated` and `payment.refunded`; do not look for Xendit's `refund.succeeded` or `refund.failed` in the PayMongo dashboard. Keep `PAYMONGO_WEBHOOK_SECRET` configured. For Xendit, add `refund.succeeded` and `refund.failed` to `/api/payments/xendit/webhook` using the existing `XENDIT_WEBHOOK_TOKEN`. PayMongo's event list is documented at https://docs.paymongo.com/reference/webhook-resource.
3. Keep `GMAIL_USER` and `GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD` configured. Set `APP_PUBLIC_URL` to the public frontend origin so emails open the customer's reservation. Email delivery retries persist in MongoDB; SMTP acceptance is at least once and a crash after acceptance can cause a duplicate email.
4. For `node server.js`, the existing minute scheduler processes queued refunds and emails. For Vercel or another serverless host, configure a durable scheduler to call **GET `/api/jobs/reservations` every minute or every five minutes**, with `Authorization: Bearer <CRON_SECRET>`. Store the same strong `CRON_SECRET` in the backend environment and scheduler's secret store. A deployment alone does not configure this external schedule. Without it, failed email deliveries, large notice batches and missing webhooks cannot be retried while the serverless function is idle. The endpoint rejects requests when the secret is absent. Normal actions and webhooks also attempt immediate delivery of up to five queued emails.

No new frontend secret or payment SDK is required. MongoDB transactions require the existing replica-set deployment. The notification and booking indexes are declared in their Mongoose schemas; installations that disable automatic indexes must create them during deployment.

## Refund processing and recovery

- PayMongo refund amounts are sent in centavos; Xendit uses PHP amounts. Both verify the reservation's original paid resource before refunding it, and return money through the original method.
- A persisted, atomic claim allows only one automatic refund POST per request. Xendit also receives the stable idempotency key. Timeouts and ambiguous responses are **never blindly retried**. The worker reads the refund resource/history, matching the request marker, original payment, currency and exact amount. Provider events are authenticated and trigger provider reads rather than trusting the event's claimed status.
- `processing` keeps the payment ledger unchanged. `completed` requires a verified provider success or a staff-recorded full manual return. Duplicate callbacks cannot add the refund twice or create another completion notification.
- `review_required` means the submission outcome is unknown. In Bookings, use **Check provider status**. Staff must reconcile with the provider before returning additional money. The provider request marker is displayed there and uses `rv-closure-<UUID>`; PayMongo also stores it in `metadata.riverview_refund_request` and notes `Riverview venue closure <requestId>`. If the provider cannot confirm the outcome, contact provider support. Do not issue a second refund or edit the booking ledger to bypass this state.
- `manual_required` or `failed` means staff must return the money. Use **Record manual refund** only after the transfer or cash return has actually happened. A mixed online/cash payment returns only the original online portion automatically; staff returns the rest. The final completion message distinguishes those portions.
- A succeeded provider refund means it was sent to the payment partner, not necessarily posted in the customer's account. The UI uses provider-specific estimates and does not promise a 30–60 minute return. PayMongo wallets generally post within 24 hours after processing; cards may take up to 30 days. Xendit posting time depends on the bank or wallet.

Provider references: [PayMongo refunds](https://docs.paymongo.com/docs/payment-acceptance-refunds), [PayMongo refund resource](https://docs.paymongo.com/reference/refund-resource), [Xendit refund payment](https://docs.xendit.co/docs/refund-payment-request).

## Additive storage and rollback

Bookings gain optional `venueClosure` and `closureRefund` subdocuments. Existing booking records, statuses, amounts and deposit fields remain readable without a data rewrite. `Notification` stores user-scoped read state, an optional reservation snapshot for email rendering, and the durable email outbox. Closure, cancellation, refund ledger and notification writes commit together; an email or provider API call is made only after that transaction commits.

Do not roll back to code that ignores pending closures or refunds while those jobs are unresolved: its no-show worker could penalize affected guests, and its manual refund controls would lack the online refund guard. Resolve/reconcile outstanding jobs first, then stop the worker and deploy the earlier version. Retain the additive data and provider references for audit; never delete payment history to roll back.

## Verification

`node --test` in BackEnd verifies affected/overnight bookings, full refunds, repeated requests and webhooks, ownership, failed and timed-out submissions, cash remainders, closure reschedule exceptions and actual provider request formats with mocked network/SMTP. Frontend reservation status and receipt tests cover closure/no-show and refund copy. Live money transfers and real customer emails are not part of automated verification.
